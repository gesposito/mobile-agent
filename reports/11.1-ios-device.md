# 11.1: physical iPhone run

Tasks 7.1 to 7.3 of `physical-ios` ask for the harness on a physical iPhone: every primitive and the three reference flows through `mobile-agent`, 50 measured iterations after 3 warm-up, judged against the `ios-device` gates (7.1); the same flows through the baselines task 6.3 brought up on the phone, with the hand-offs between them (7.2); and what the device changes for the mirror, from the raw data (7.3).

This report records those runs on 2026-10-10 on an iPhone SE (3rd generation) with iOS 26.6.2. Times are UTC. The harness's reports are under `reports/11.1-ios-device/`, one directory per phase (`a-swiftui-primitives/` to `n-expo-big-screen-baselines/`, with the reruns beside them), and the probes of 7.3 under `probes/`. A number below cites the file it comes from by its path under that directory.

## Verdict

- **Gates** (7.1, 50 measured iterations after 3 warm-up, each at p95; `a-swiftui-primitives/`, `e-expo-primitives/`). All four pass on both fixtures:
  - CLI start, 10.0 and 5.0 ms against 20 ms;
  - cold attach, the runner installed, 2,603.1 ms (SwiftUI) and 2,804.1 ms (Expo) against 5,000 ms;
  - warm snapshot of the 500-cell Big tab, 13.7 and 11.1 ms against 1,000 ms;
  - tap dispatch through the host's `dtuhidd`, 55.4 and 54.9 ms against 150 ms;
  - tap to frame change, reported with no verdict: 360.1 and 241.6 ms.
  - The warm snapshot passes on the daemon's replica, as on the Simulator. The runner's read of the same screens, 450 to 650 ms, is what the 1 s limit was set from, and no warm snapshot waits for it (finding 1).
- **The comparison.** On the phone, `mobile-agent`'s flows took 1.3 to 2.0 times the Simulator's at p50: login 6,662.1 and 6,262.7 ms, list-scroll 32,100.5 and 27,462.0 ms, big-screen 4,690.0 and 3,658.4 ms on SwiftUI and Expo. WebDriverAgent was faster than `mobile-agent` on every flow it timed: 1.13 and 1.10 times on login, 2.92 and 2.66 times on list-scroll, whose 9 swipes beat 22 to 25 resting strokes that each settle and read, and 1.35 times on SwiftUI's big-screen over the 6 iterations its session lasted. agent-device was slower than `mobile-agent` on login, by 1.70 and 1.73 times, and faster on SwiftUI's big-screen, by 1.34 times; its list-scroll failed its golden check on both fixtures, as task 6.3 found. On the Simulator `mobile-agent` had been 2.27 and 1.24 times faster than agent-device on those two SwiftUI flows (`reports/10.2-simulator.md`). Expo's big-screen through the baselines could not run: the phone refused the Expo build the harness had installed again, until it verifies the developer online (Baselines). `mobile-agent`'s own flows beside the baselines lost the runner's connection in every phase from `i` to `m` and failed 86 to 100 % of their iterations, so its numbers here are 7.1's, run alone (finding 5).
- **What the device changes for the mirror** (7.3, What the device changes for the mirror):
  - a read of the 500-cell tab costs 450 to 650 ms, against 150 ms on the Simulator; the warm snapshot never pays it, and the first snapshot after a relaunch pays the device listing (177.0 ms on SwiftUI) or the listing and the tail of a read (558.8 ms on Expo) rather than a read (findings 1 and 3);
  - a tap's first changed frame comes 340.0 and 219.6 ms after the dispatch at p50, 4.7 and 4.6 times the Simulator's, and its first tree delta 592.6 and 347.1 ms (finding 8);
  - settle ended on frames on SwiftUI in 50 of 50 and on the tree on Expo in 44 of 50, as on the Simulator, in 350.4 and 185.3 ms at p50;
  - the idle poll keeps the target's worker reading about half the time, so a command waits up to 733 ms behind it, as every other SwiftUI screenshot did (finding 2);
  - of task 6.1's three tree-read findings, `row-12` is the row the SE's floating tab bar covers (finding 6), the alert's message is missing from both platforms' listings (finding 7), and the warm snapshot is served from the replica (finding 1).
- **The host and the phone.** Other sessions' builds kept the 1-minute load past 4 for minutes in most phases, and the host held process launches for up to 130.6 s (findings 4 and 10). The runner's connection was lost in six phases (`h2`, `i` to `m`) and in a probe, after which the daemon kept the target disconnected until it stopped (finding 5); WebDriverAgent lost its session the same way in two phases, and agent-device recovered. After the harness installed the Expo build again at 12:29, the phone refused to launch it ("Unable to Verify App: An internet connection is required to verify trust of the developer"), which ended the run (The phone afterwards).

### Method

Each phase was one harness run with its own `--out`, so that each phase's load is on record and a disturbed phase could run again:

```sh
mobile-agent-bench run --target <udid> --cli <repo>/target/release/mobile-agent --iterations 50 --warmup 3 \
  --auto-lock-off --fixture swiftui --ios-app swiftui=fixtures/swiftui/build/Build/Products/Debug-iphoneos/FixtureSwiftUI.app \
  --no-baselines --primitive cli-start --primitive cold-attach … --primitive settle --out <dir>      # a-swiftui-primitives
# the same with --flow login, --flow list-scroll or --flow big-screen in place of the primitives (b, c, d),
# with --fixture expo --ios-app expo=fixtures/expo/build/device/build/Build/Products/Release-iphoneos/FixtureExpo.app (e to h),
# and without --no-baselines for the flows beside the baselines (i to n)
```

A phase's window runs from the end of its quiet-host wait to the bench's exit; each `report.json` has the bench's own start and finish and the load at its start. The flow phases' gate tables list every gate as not measured, which exits 0.

- **Quiet host.** Before each phase a script waited for a 1-minute load under 4 and no `cargo`, `rustc`, `xcodebuild`, `java`, `gradle`, `kotlinc`, `qemu-system`, `emulator` or `Simulator` process above 5% of a core, checking every 60 s for up to 45 minutes; every phase found one within 1 to 7 checks. The load came from other sessions' Rust builds and tests on the same host, in bursts that took the 1-minute load from under 3 to as much as 26 within a minute, so a phase that started quiet could still run through one. A sampler recorded the load, the three busiest processes and the command each tool was running every 20 s (the counts in the table). A phase whose load stayed at 4 or more for more than a few minutes was to run again where the time allowed. Expo's big-screen flow ran twice more, `h2` and `h3`, since the load held its first run `h` at 4 or more for most of its 4 minutes. `a2`, `e2` and `g2`, the later primitives of `a` and `e` and Expo's list-scroll, did not run: the load did not subside, `m` ran at a median of 6.44, and from 12:29 the phone refused the Expo build (The phone afterwards).
- **Launch stalls.** The host held process launches for long stretches at times: a probe timing `security find-identity` and `/usr/bin/true` every 5 s from 10:31 to 11:07 (the former is the call the iOS driver makes on every device listing) saw 17 of 640 launches take over a second, up to 130.6 s (`probes/launches.txt`), and the sampler's own 20 s steps stretched to as much as 2 min 21 s in the same windows. Every CLI command is a new process, so such a stall lands in whatever series runs then (finding 4).
- **Counts.** 50 measured iterations after 3 warm-up and 1 retry everywhere, the baselines included: the longest phase with the baselines, `i` at 25 min 42 s, stayed under the 2 hours past which the plan allowed 20 after 2.
- **Gates.** The harness's defaults for `ios-device` (`crates/bench/README.md`, Gates), each at p95: CLI start under 20 ms, cold attach under 5,000 ms with the runner installed, warm snapshot of a fixture's Big screen under 1,000 ms, tap dispatch under 150 ms through the host's input path, and tap to frame change reported with no verdict.
- **Reports.** `report.json` and `report.md` of each phase are as the bench wrote them but for three placeholders: the phone's identifier is written as `IPHONE-SE-UDID`, the checkout as `<repo>` and the home directory as `$HOME` (error messages that name the CLI's and agent-device's paths), the same text replaced in both files, so they still agree. The baselines' flow files are beside them under `flows/` with the identifier in their names replaced the same way; Appium's and the WebDriverAgent helper's logs and the CLI adapter's screenshots are not committed.

## Findings

Findings 1 to 5 concern `mobile-agent` on the phone, 6 to 8 the tree-read findings of task 6.1 and the frames, 9 the baselines and 10 the host. Each is a follow-up in design.md's Open Questions.

### 1. The warm snapshot gate measures the replica, and the first snapshot measures the listing

- **Measured.** The warm snapshot of the Big tab took 7.6 and 7.5 ms at p50 and 13.7 and 11.1 ms at p95, against the 1,000 ms gate set from the runner's 450 to 550 ms read (`a-swiftui-primitives/report.json`, `e-expo-primitives/report.json`). The first snapshot after a relaunch took 177.0 ms on SwiftUI and 558.8 ms on Expo at p50, and every probe snapshot served from the replica reported a `readMs` under 1.5 ms (`probes/reads-swiftui.jsonl`, `probes/reads-expo.jsonl`).
- **What each carries.** The warm snapshot carries nothing of the device, as on the Simulator (`reports/10.2-simulator.md`, Gates). The first carries the device listing a command makes when the last one is older than 2 s (finding 3), and on Expo the tail of a read the worker began as `open` returned. Neither is the runner's read, which the gate's limit describes; that read showed only where a snapshot met it: 665.0 and 562.2 ms (the series' maxima), 438.3 and 446.8 ms (`probes/idle-expo.jsonl`).
- **Follow-up.** The harness could record a snapshot's `readMs` and `servedFromReplica` beside its wall time, and the device gate could be judged on a read the daemon makes, so that the 1 s limit describes what it measures.

### 2. The idle poll keeps the target's worker reading about half the time

- **Measured.** With nothing driving the phone, the worker was reading when 60% of the probe's SwiftUI snapshots arrived and 47% of its Expo ones; those waited 160 to 733 ms (`probes/idle-swiftui.jsonl`, `probes/idle-expo.jsonl`). The screenshot primitive, one capture per iteration, waited behind the poll's read in 27 of 50 iterations on SwiftUI and 18 of 50 on Expo: 843.6 and 214.9 ms at p50, 968.1 and 776.2 ms at p95, against 178 to 254 ms for a capture that met no read (`a-swiftui-primitives/report.json`, `e-expo-primitives/report.json`).
- **Why.** The mirror polls a window without events 1 s after its last read began (`DEFAULT_POLL_INTERVAL`), and a read of the 500-cell tab takes 450 to 650 ms on the phone, against 150 ms on the Simulator, so the poll that costs the Simulator 15% of the worker's time costs the phone 45 to 65%, and every command meets it at random.
- **Follow-up.** design.md's open question on the idle poll (task 5.2) has its numbers: a poll interval that grows with the read's cost, or no poll while the frames show no change, would give that time back; the frames already tell a still screen.

### 3. Every command after 2 s without one lists the devices again, 150 to 220 ms on an iPhone

- **Measured.** A snapshot 2.5 s after the last command waited 182.2 to 226.0 ms on SwiftUI with nothing read (`probes/reads-swiftui.jsonl`); one within 2 s, 6.0 to 10.0 ms. The first snapshot after a relaunch, whose `open` took 2.5 to 3.7 s, waited 146.5 to 153.5 ms.
- **Why.** `Registry::resolve` asks the drivers again when its listing is 2 s old (`DISCOVERY_TTL`), and the iOS driver's listing probes lockdown and runs `security find-identity` and `security find-certificate` for the signing identity: 162.7 and 177.2 ms at p50 and p95 with the SE in task 5.1, 45 ms of it `security`.
- **Follow-up.** A target that is attached needs no readiness probe to be resolved, and the signing identity does not change between commands: caching the identity check, or resolving an attached target without listing, would take this off every agent's first command after a pause.

### 4. Launch stalls on the host reach the daemon's commands through that listing

- **Measured.** Twice in the primitives and flows alone a command got no answer from the daemon within 30 s (`daemon_timeout`): a `wait --settle` in SwiftUI's tap-dispatch series and a `click` in SwiftUI's login, whose retry then took 30.2 s (`a-swiftui-primitives/report.json`, `b-swiftui-login/report.json`), and once more in Expo's big-screen rerun, 37.5 s (`h3-expo-big-screen/report.json`). Three Expo list-scroll iterations took 56.6 to 73.7 s with the same 22 strokes as the others' 26.1 to 28.9 s, and set that series' p95 (`g-expo-list-scroll/report.json`). A probe timing `security find-identity -v -p codesigning` and `/usr/bin/true` every 5 s from 10:31 to 11:07, beside phases `h3` and `i`, saw 17 of its 640 launches take over a second, up to 130.6 s and 17.7 s, against medians of 44.6 and 5.6 ms (`probes/launches.txt`).
- **Why, as far as it was traced.** The host's endpoint security inspects process starts, and at times held them. The bench starts a process per command, and the iOS driver's listing (finding 3) starts two `security` processes on the daemon's request path, synchronously and with no timeout (`crates/ios/src/identity.rs`), so a held launch holds the command. Which stall met which iteration was not traced: the samples carry no times.
- **Follow-up.** The same cache as finding 3 takes the daemon off this path; a timeout on the identity check would bound it otherwise.

### 5. After a lost runner connection the daemon keeps the target disconnected until it stops

- **Measured.** In the first Expo big-screen rerun, 27 iterations passed; then the runner's connection fell silent (`nothing arrived from the other side for 3.0 s`), one attempt found lockdown not answering within 10 s and one CLI command ran out its 120 s, and every later attempt, 49 of them over 26 iterations, failed at once with the same `disconnected` from the same daemon, so 52% of the series failed (`h2-expo-big-screen/report.json`); the phone was ready and attached again in 4.3 s six minutes later (`probes/attaches.txt`). In every flow phase beside the baselines, `i` to `m`, `mobile-agent`'s series lost the connection the same way after 1 to 10 iterations, in four of them a 30 s daemon timeout first and in `l` a 10 s wait for the welcome, and failed 86 to 100 % of its iterations, while the next phase's daemon attached at once (`i-swiftui-login-baselines/` to `m-expo-list-scroll-baselines/`). The read-cost probe met the same loss on Expo's idle Big tab: a snapshot got no answer within 30 s, the next two failed `disconnected`, `status` said the runner `failed, 0 restarts` with 10.8 captures a second still arriving, and the runner's process stayed on the phone until the daemon's `shutdown` ended it (`probes/reads-expo.jsonl`, `probes/status-after-stall.txt`).
- **Why.** The mirror stops reading once its backend reports `disconnected` and refuses actions until the target is attached again (`crates/mirror/README.md`), and the daemon's worker attaches a target once and detaches it only when the daemon stops or a command panics (`crates/daemon/src/worker.rs`). The driver's own containment starts over on a new attach (task 3.6), which this path never reaches. The runner's restart after the loss failed, likely because the silent runner still held the phone's automation session. What silenced it was not found; the first loss fell in a window where the run's own load sampler stalled for 2 min 21 s, as it did when the host held launches (finding 10), and the probe's had no sampler running.
- **Follow-up.** The worker could detach a target whose driver reports a lost connection, so that the next command attaches again and ends the old runner first, as an attach already does with a runner left from a lost session.

### 6. `row-12` is the row the SE's floating tab bar covers

As The three tree-read findings of task 6.1 has it: the cell at 592 to 644 points lies under the tab bar at 588 to 642, and XCTest's snapshot leaves it out (`probes/rowbox.txt`, `probes/list-swiftui.jsonl`, `probes/list-swiftui.jpg`). Nothing tappable is missing. Follow-up: none beyond noting it in the fixture's README, since any screen with a floating bar does the same.

### 7. An alert's message is missing from both platforms' listings

The in-app alert's title and button are listed and its message is not, on the phone (`probes/alert-swiftui.jsonl`, `probes/alert-expo.jsonl`) and in the Simulator's listing task 4.4 recorded (`crates/ios/tests/fixtures/listings/swiftui-alert.txt`). Follow-up: find where the conversion or the listing drops the message, a scroll view's text inside the alert, on the Simulator, where the reader's own alert model has it.

### 8. A tap's first changed frame comes 220 to 340 ms after its dispatch

- **Measured.** 340.0 and 360.1 ms at p50 and p95 on SwiftUI, 219.6 and 241.6 ms on Expo, from the dispatch, 166 to 286 ms at p50 after the acknowledgement (`a-swiftui-primitives/report.json`, `e-expo-primitives/report.json`), against 72.8 and 47.7 ms on the Simulator. Spike S11 measured 274 and 439 ms after the acknowledgement for a row with one poller, 241 and 309 ms with two.
- **Follow-up.** Design D23 gets these numbers beside S11's; the gate stays reported until a frame path at 30 a second exists (AVFoundation once the camera permission is granted, or the display stream on iOS 27).

### 9. agent-device's list-scroll on the SE

- **Measured.** agent-device's golden check of list-scroll failed on both fixtures, on both tries each, with `Batch failed at step 3 (scroll): scroll down spent its 12-pass budget without id="row-200" becoming visible`, so the flow was not timed (`j-swiftui-list-scroll-baselines/report.json`, `m-expo-list-scroll-baselines/report.json`), as in task 6.3's functional run and on the Simulator's SwiftUI fixture (`reports/10.2-simulator.md`, finding 3). `mobile-agent`'s `scroll --until` took 25 resting strokes on SwiftUI and 22 or 23 on Expo to bring `row-200` into view on the SE's 667-point screen, and WebDriverAgent 9 swipes.
- **Follow-up.** None for `mobile-agent`; agent-device's budget is fixed (task 6.3 found its suggested step of 0.8 of the viewport failing too), and the comparison records list-scroll as not timed for it.

### 10. The run's host

- **Load.** Other sessions' builds and tests took the 1-minute load past 4 in every phase but `b`, `d` and `n2` at some point, up to 26 within a minute of a quiet check, and held it there for most of `e`, `h`, `h2`, `h3` and `m` (Method). Of those phases only Expo's big-screen ran again (Method, Quiet host).
- **Launches.** Process launches were held for up to 130.6 s at times (finding 4). Three of the longest holds lasted 130.4, 130.6 and 125.0 s, about the same length, as a timeout would (`probes/launches.txt`).
- **What it means for the numbers.** The medians are stable from phase to phase and rerun to rerun (the tables' raw files); the p95 and maximum of a flow can carry a stall, as Expo's list-scroll's did, and the tables say where.
