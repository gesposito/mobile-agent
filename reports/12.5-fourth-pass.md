# 12.5: The fourth demo pass on real apps, and what CI's fresh devices sorted

Task 4.1 of `pass-4-fixes` asks for the public record of the pass on real apps and the site's recording runs from which the change came: each task's counts beside the third pass's, the failed calls and their causes, the gaps the pass and the runs found, what CI's fresh devices sorted, the fixes that landed and what remains; the notes and transcripts kept privately.

This report records the pass of 2026-10-30, from 19:14 to 21:25 UTC, and what the change made of it up to 2026-11-05: the setup, the nine tasks against the third pass, the failed calls, the gaps with the lines that showed them, CI's verdicts on its fresh devices, the bugs found on the way, the fixes with their pull requests and proof runs, what remains and what was not measured. Times are UTC. The pass is the fourth demo pass: the nine tasks of the passes of 2026-10-20, 10-22 and 10-23 through the server, on the Simulator's Settings, Calendar, Maps, Safari and Photos and the emulator's Settings, Clock, Dialer and Chrome, which the change's `proposal.md` calls the fourth pass on real apps and `README.md` counts as the fifth, after those of 2026-10-20, 10-21, 10-22 and 10-23; "the third pass" below is the demo pass of 2026-10-23, which `reports/12.4-android-pass.md` replayed. The site's runs are the project's site's recording runs on the same Mac, a re-take of its four flows on the evening of 2026-10-30 and a take of a new Safari flow on the morning of 2026-10-31, which drove the CLI by script on a Simulator of their own. The fourth pass's notes and transcripts, kept privately, as the site's recording report and its takes are, hold every number of the pass below; its counts were recounted from the transcripts with the third pass's counters and agree with the notes. CI's numbers come from the runs named by their ids, and each decision from the change's `design.md`, whose "As found" and "As built" record it. A reply is quoted as the server's text gave it, with the Simulator's identifier written `<the Simulator>` and a listing cut where `…` stands.

## Verdict

The nine tasks were done. iOS had no failed call, 64 server calls over 75 turns against the third pass's 57 and 67, the fresh Simulator's first-launch sheets and permission alerts taking calls in four of its five tasks; Android had 7 failed calls of 62 over 70 turns against 4 of 63 over 71, six of them in Chrome under the guest's "Chrome isn't responding" dialogs, on an emulator booted two minutes before at a host load of 7, and the seventh Clock's minute under the time picker's overlay, which the change before left. The host's default model had moved to `claude-opus-5-5` from the third pass's `claude-fable-5-1`, so turns, tokens and costs do not compare between the two passes, and four of the nine prompts were reconstructions; the replies compare.

- **Fixed before and seen working.** Hits without a ref named, Safari's address field among them (`hit text "Address" (no ref)`); `open` naming the permission alert in front of Maps; a twin's call in 0.79 and 1.32 s, against the third pass's 2.38 and 2.62 s; a scroll answering once the content came to rest, 1.14 s after its stroke; a settle right after Safari's Enter waiting 2.64 s for the page; Bold Text's flips as delta lines.
- **Gaps.** Nine, with the site's runs, beside the time picker's overlay: a launch the Simulator refuses right after an app closes, a read-back one key early, a settle that gives up with a success exit, a tap by coordinates named `generic`, an interactive listing empty right after an alert closes, a stale ref silent about the dialog in front, a text wait matching a longer text, a header covering a button in plain view, and a node Calendar keeps.

- **Fixed.** Every finding that reproduced, on CI, on the fakes or on this Mac, in pull requests #21 to #57; the refused launch and the short read-back, which did not reproduce on CI, gated on their case.
- **What remains.** The Expo fixture's list on iOS 27.0, whose `row-100` `scroll --until` still missed in 3 runs of 4; Calendar's kept details screen, the app's; the host's conditions under which the launch refusal, the short read-back, Photos' late change and Chrome's dialogs came; and 16 of the Simulator driver's own device tests failing on iOS 27.0, outside the change's findings.

- **Counts**, as 12.2 to 12.4 took them and through the third pass's counters: a turn is Claude Code's `num_turns`; server calls are the stream's `tool_use` blocks for the server's tools, Claude Code's own `ToolSearch` counted apart; a failed call is a `tool_result` with `is_error`; cost is `total_cost_usd`, at the model's list price; time is `duration_ms`, API time `duration_api_ms`, and tool time the sum of the calls' times from `tool_use` to `tool_result`. Chrome's run counted 32 turns under `--max-turns 30` and ended `success`, as its result line says.
- **Load.** The 1-minute load, polled every 60 s; a half started once it had been under 4 for five polls in a row. Before the iOS half the pass waited from 16:41 to 17:56, its first hour also for the site's first recording run, on a Simulator of its own, to end; the Simulator's boot took the load to 103 for a minute, and the gate after it waited from 17:57 to 19:13, 1 h 16 min, reset by other sessions' builds up to 13.2; before the Android half it waited from 19:20 to 21:20, 2 h 0 min. ### Found on the way

The first runs, 41219603093 and 41224450077, failed 30 then 29 of the Simulator's 1,804 tests and 4 then 1 of the emulator's 1,579. Task 1.2 sorted their causes, each shown on CI first by a debug run (41229820313, 41231732649):

- **The driver's runtime lookup, the tool's.** The driver made the tests' own devices on the newest runtime installed, iOS 27.1, which takes no iPhone 17 on the image, and CoreSimulator refused ten tests' devices with SimError 403; `SimulatorDriver::create_device_like` creates them on the runtime of the device under test (#25).
- **The Expo fixture's scene life cycle, the fixture's.** Built with the iOS 27.1 SDK, the Expo fixture trapped at launch on iOS 27.0 in UIKit's check for apps that do not adopt the scene life cycle, which Expo SDK 57's template does not; a local config plugin adopts it, the same bundle launching on iOS 27.0 and 26.5 (#28).
- **The runner's.** The tests' millisecond and frame-rate bounds missed under a load of 17 to 19 on 3 cores, so the 13 tests that assert one print it under `MOBILE_AGENT_HOST_GATES=off` and assert the order of events and the fields (#27).
- **The test's.** The raw gRPC screenshot equals `screencap` under software rendering; the full runs had compared two reads of a screen in transition, so the test compares across a still screen (#26).

The Simulator job then outgrew 90 minutes and runs within 150 (#30). Task 1.1 found beside them that the benchmarks workflow's emulator had no device profile, a 320x640 display at 160 dpi, which `ci-benchmarks` gave the Pixel 8's (#23).
