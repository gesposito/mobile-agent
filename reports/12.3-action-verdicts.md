### Method

- **The exchanges.** A client of this replay's own, in Python, started the server for each exchange and spoke to it over its standard input and output, as 12.2's replay did: `initialize` at the protocol revision 2025-06-18 and `notifications/initialized`, then each call as a `tools/call` line once the call before it had its answer. It recorded every message with its time and each answer's text as it landed; a call's time below is the client's clock from the request to its answer. Each exchange is a fresh server, so a fresh session whose first snapshot is the whole listing, as each of the demo pass's tasks was a fresh `claude -p`, and its first `open` named the device, as the demo pass's model did.
- **The calls** are the demo pass's model's where its transcripts hold them: Photos' `scroll` down 700 points and up 700 before `click text=Favorites`; Maps' `fill` by the ref the first listing gave the search box, with the text the demo pass searched for, Lisbon; Safari's `snapshot`, `click #TabBarItemTitle`, `type` and `press` Enter before the settle. Settings' rounds and the flips of Bold Text by its text are this replay's. Each case ends with what shows the calls' effect: a settle and a whole listing, a `get`, or a screenshot written to a file.
- **Runs.** Photos, Maps and Safari three times each; Settings' `press home` and `open` in one exchange of five rounds; the delta once as the case reads and once with a settle after each push, the guide's loop; and the variants the findings called for: Photos with a settle between the scroll and the click, three times, and Safari's menu in four exchanges. Each exchange's app was terminated through `simctl` before it, so that its `open` launched the app.
- **The starting state**, set through the CLI in a session of its own before the first exchange: Safari's one tab, which task 2.3's checks had left on example.org, closed from the tab overview, whose tip went first, so that Safari opened on its start page as in the demo pass, and the tab each Safari exchange opened closed the same way before the next; Settings at its root with Bold Text off; Photos on Collections, where Pinned holds Favorites and Albums holds none; Maps on its search card, with Lisbon among the recents from the demo pass.
- **The counted run**, the demo pass's command: `claude -p "<task>" --mcp-config mcp.json --strict-mcp-config --allowedTools "mcp__mobile-agent__*" --max-turns 30 --output-format stream-json --verbose < /dev/null`, from the socket directory, with `mcp.json` holding the server's entry and the environment above. The task is the demo pass's Photos task word for word, recovered from the client's log of that run: "Use only the mobile-agent MCP server's tools. On the iOS Simulator, open the Photos app (com.apple.mobileslideshow), open the first album in its albums or collections section, and say what the album is called and how many items it shows. Finally, report the outcome in one or two lines, and which tool calls failed, if any." Photos was terminated before it, and the daemon was already running.
- **Counts**, as 12.2 took them and through the same summary for the three passes' Photos runs: a turn is Claude Code's `num_turns`; server calls are the stream's `tool_use` blocks for the server's tools, Claude Code's own `ToolSearch` counted apart; a failed call is a `tool_result` with `is_error`; tokens are the result line's `usage`; cost is `total_cost_usd`, at the model's list price; time is the client's `duration_ms`.
- **Verification** after the counted run, in a CLI session of its own on the same socket directory: the whole listing as the run left it, and the daemon's `status` for the server's session; then `close --keep`.
- **Load.** Each exchange and the counted run started once the 1-minute load average had been under 6, polled every 60 s, at 3.36 to 5.97, but for Photos' second run, which started at 8.82, the script having printed the load without waiting on it; its replies are those of the other two runs. ## Findings

### 1. A click right after a plain scroll meets a list in motion

`scroll` without `until` strokes as a finger that lifts while it moves (`Release::Moving` in `crates/daemon/src/handlers/act.rs`), so the content flings on after the strokes, and the reply comes once the strokes went in. On Photos' Collections the list moved on for about a second after `scroll up 700` answered: a settle right after it lasted 887.87 to 1200.16 ms with frames changing last. A click right after the scroll went in while the list moved, and the album did not open, three times in three, as in the demo pass; once the list had come to rest, the same click opened the album three times in three. The reply named `hit button "Favorites"` either way: the element the replica held under the point at dispatch, which is what D1 takes. The changes the click reported, a frame change at 10.9 to 31.8 ms and a tree delta at 131.5 to 138.0 ms, came from the list in motion, so nothing in the line told a tap the list took from one the row took; the settle after it and the listing did. A model following the guide settles between the two, as the variant did; the demo pass's model did not, and nor would one that takes `✓ Done (2 strokes)` for a list at rest. The scroll could reply once the content has come to rest, or the click could say that the screen was moving at its dispatch: task 3.4.

### 2. A settle can end before the tree holds Safari's menu

The listing right after a settle on the tap that opens Safari's More menu held no interactive element in 2 of 9 opens, the preparation's through the CLI, after a settle of 1005.39 ms, and the first round of the server's check, after one of 1038.86 ms, whose second listing 41 ms later was the same; the seven other opens listed the menu's seven buttons at once after settles of 1072.07 to 1338.73 ms, and the next CLI listing after the failed one listed them too. That is the demo pass's menu case (the change's `proposal.md`), whose settle took 989 ms. Each settle ended with frames changing last, so the input's first effect had come and the quiet window of 200 ms had passed. The replica re-reads a Simulator's tree after each injected action, when frames change and on a 1 s poll (`README.md`, Snapshots), so a tree that the app completes after its frames went still would reach the replica at that poll, which can come after the quiet window. When the buttons reach the tree, and which read brings them, is for task 3.5 to measure before it changes the settle.

### 3. A click on Safari's address bar names no element

`click #TabBarItemTitle` answered `✓ Done (dispatch 0.1 ms, first frame change 289.8 ms, first tree delta 363.9 ms)` with no `hit`: on the start page the address bar is a plain text without a ref (12.2, finding 5), and `hit` names the top-most element with a ref under the point, null where none lies there (D1, As built). The tap focused the field, as `type`'s read-back showed. The line names nothing where the listing names nothing; not a gap.

### 4. A listing taken during a push renews every ref after it

In the delta case's run without settles, the listing taken right after the click that pushed Display & Text Size came during the push, with rows counted under an overlay that no listing after the push held; once the push ended every row bore a fresh ref, so the snapshot after the first flip came back as the whole listing, a delta being 70% of it or more (`README.md`, Delta snapshots), and only the second flip's came as lines. With a settle after each push, both flips came as lines. That is the guide's loop, which settles before it lists; not a gap.

### 5. A click on a switch row by its text names the switch it pressed

`click text=Bold Text` answered `✓ Done (hit switch, dispatch 45.3 ms, ...)`: on a Simulator the press goes to the control of the switch the row holds (real-app-fixes, design D6), which has no name, so the line gives its role alone, and no warning, since the control lies in the switch the selector named. A model reads that a switch took the press; the flip itself shows in the delta and in `get value`.
