## Findings

### 1. `get value` by the identifier a switch shares with its cell answers `no value`

Settings' Bold Text row is a table cell around a labelled switch, which holds its label and its control; the cell, the switch and the label share `ENHANCE_TEXT_LEGIBILITY` (D6, As built). `click #ENHANCE_TEXT_LEGIBILITY` presses the switch's control through D6's clause for an element that holds a switch sharing its identifier; `get value #ENHANCE_TEXT_LEGIBILITY` reads the cell, which the selector names first, and answered `no value` with the switch on, while `text=Bold Text` read `off`, `on` and `off` in the run; in the CLI's checks, with the switch off, the switch's ref and its control's read `off` and the identifier `no value` again, the JSON's `value` null.
