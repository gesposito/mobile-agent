# Tasks

Rules that bind every task below (see design.md, Delivery process): fail-first tests on fixtures of the shapes the smoke test found, red recorded then green; atomic commits without trailers; the validation-and-fix phase before the next task; installs through `sfw`; fixtures carry placeholder names only. Tasks 1.1 to 1.6 touch disjoint files where marked and can run as separate implementers; 2.1 after them; 2.2 is the acceptance run by the other session.

## 1. Fixes

- [ ] 1.1 Covers from the drawing order on iOS (`crates/mirror`: `view.rs`, a fixture `consent-ios.json` with a heading inside a fixed footer's button, `create-ios.json` with a floating button over a list's last row, `feed-ios.json` with a sticky header over a scrolled card): failing tests that the heading, the row and the card are left out of the interactive listing, that `visibility` reports them covered, and that acting on their refs fails with `element_covered` naming the cover; then apply the `on_top` rule to iOS nodes without a flag, judged by the siblings' order and bounds when the cover shows something; verify red-then-green, `cargo test -p mirror` green, and the mirror's microbenchmarks for `snapshot/interactive-*` re-run on a quiet host with the baseline refreshed if they moved past the threshold

- [ ] 1.3 Read-back through descendants (`crates/daemon/src/handlers/act.rs` and the fake app's fields): failing tests that `fill` on a composite field of six boxes verifies through their texts, that a field with no value to read succeeds with `verified: false` and `no_value_to_read`, and that a differing value still fails with `text_mismatch` after the retry; then implement the descendant read-back and the unverified outcome; verify red-then-green
