- [x] 1.1 Write `docs/research/ci-benchmarks.md`: how projects compare benchmarks in CI (criterion's baselines and `cargo-criterion`, interleaved A/B runs, the hosted services CodSpeed, Bencher and github-action-benchmark with what each measures and costs), what is known of GitHub's hosted runners' noise and core counts, KVM on Ubuntu runners and the Android emulator runner action, Simulators on macOS runners, the per-minute prices and an estimate of the weekly schedule's cost; what this change reuses (`just micro`'s guard and reports, the harness's adapters and provenance) and where it differs (relative judgement between refs, ratios on runners) (written on 2026-10-15 and added to `docs/research/README.md`, versions and prices as of that day: Context7 served criterion, now indexed at `criterion-rs/criterion.rs`, critcmp, Bencher, GitHub Actions and GitHub's REST API; CodSpeed's site, the READMEs of github-action-benchmark, android-emulator-runner and the runner images at pinned commits, GitHub's billing pages and changelog, and the papers' abstracts served the rest, each section saying which. criterion 0.8.2 compares one run with one stored run in one `$CRITERION_HOME` at a 1% noise threshold; cargo-criterion 1.1.0, of 2021, has no baselines; critcmp 0.1.8 compares two saved baselines with a threshold; none runs rounds. Laaber et al. (EMSE 2019) measured CVs of 0.03% to over 100% in three clouds and detected slowdowns of 10% or less with test and control interleaved on the same instances; RMIT, duet benchmarking (2.3 to 82.4 times the accuracy) and MooBench on GitHub's runners (changes down to 4.41% for sequential work) agree that pairing on one machine is what works there, while CodSpeed found jobs of one label on two CPU models, which moved `malloc` and 8 benchmarks, so runs across jobs are a trend, never a verdict. Runners: `ubuntu-latest` 4 vCPUs public, 2 private; `macos-latest` and `xcode-27` 3 M1 cores, no nested virtualization; KVM on standard Linux runners since 2024-04-02 through a udev rule, and android-emulator-runner v2.38.0 with API 36's `google_apis` x86_64 image. Simulators: `macos-latest` has Xcode 26.6 with iOS 26.5 but the driver's keys and text need Xcode 27's CoreSimulator, and `xcode-27` has Xcode 27.0 with iOS 27.0 only, so neither has the validated pair and the Simulator job needs `xcode-27`, a change to D4 made in task 3.2. Services: CodSpeed (free for open source and up to 5 users, $15 a user, instruction-count simulation or bare-metal macro runners), Bencher (free for public projects, Pro $100 a month, bare metal $1.00 an hour) and github-action-benchmark (a `gh-pages` history, alert at 200%) all build history from per-push runs and need an account, a token or a branch, so none is adopted and CodSpeed's simulation stays D2's alternative. Prices since 2026-01-01: $0.006 a Linux minute, $0.062 a macOS minute, included minutes consumed at list price, public repositories free; the estimate is 238 minutes and $6.58 a weekly run, about $29 a month in a private repository, 86% of it macOS, $1.88 for a micro dispatch. The threshold question: the sources support 5 to 10% only with both sides on one machine, so 10% holds until S12.
