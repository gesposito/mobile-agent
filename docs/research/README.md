# Research notes

State-of-the-art notes that open each task group. Per design.md's Delivery process, no implementation task in a group starts before its note exists.

Each note names what already exists, what this project reuses, where this design differs, and the sources, and is linked from design.md once reviewed. Notes are named by topic, for example `android-agent.md`, `simulator.md` and `protocol.md` (tasks 2.1 to 2.3 of the archived change `mobile-automation-foundation`), `microbenchmarks.md` (task 1.1 of `microbenchmarks`), on harness libraries, noise control and continuous benchmarking, `ios-device.md` (task 1.1 of `physical-ios`), on physical iPhones: the device services and their transports, the tools that drive devices, the `idevice` crate, the frame paths and signing, `ci-benchmarks.md` (task 1.1 of `ci-benchmarks`), on benchmarking in CI: criterion's baselines and comparing two builds on one machine, the hosted services, GitHub's runners with their noise, KVM for the emulator and Xcode for the Simulator, and the prices, `open-sourcing.md` (task 1.1 of `open-sourcing`), on making the repository public: Astral's CI and release practices, OpenSSF Scorecard and the best-practices badge, GitHub's community standards and its procedure for removing data from a repository and its caches, cargo-deny, cargo-about and Dependabot's cooldown, and what a first release will need, and `agent-surfaces.md` (opening the change that adds agent surfaces, before its proposal), on the surfaces beside the CLI: the Model Context Protocol's 2026-07-28 revision and what Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Claude Desktop do with a server, the rmcp SDK and schemas from the daemon's types, the mobile MCP servers and what their users report, agent-browser's MCP server, providers and plugins, and a CDP subset.

`docs/overview.md` draws on these notes, the spike reports and the end-to-end reports to answer how mobile-agent differs from the tools the notes survey, where it is faster, and what it supports and leaves out.
