mobile-agent: a guide for agents

mobile-agent drives Android devices and emulators, iOS Simulators and iPhones.
A daemon mirrors each screen; the grammar, refs and JSON follow agent-browser.

The loop
  1. mobile-agent snapshot -i       list actionable elements with their refs
  2. mobile-agent click @e3         act on a ref from that snapshot
  3. mobile-agent wait --settle     let the screen settle after the action
  4. mobile-agent snapshot -i       snapshot again before the next action

Snapshots
  - button "Sign in" [id=login-submit, ref=e4]
  One element per line: role, name in quotes, attributes, the ref last.
  A field's value follows ": ". A line in parentheses, as "(8 more actionable
  items above, 2 of them behind the header, scroll up to reveal)", counts what
  is scrolled away, covered or under the keyboard, which covers what it draws:
  nothing if a hardware keyboard shows no keys; iOS's edit menu only its strip.
  -i leaves out what a tap reaches none of, as under a header or a footer;
  snapshot lists it at visible=0% unless a dialog covers it. Both say
  visible=35% of what a tap reaches part of. Every tab has a ref.
  snapshot -i        only elements with refs; start here
  snapshot           every element, texts and system bars too
  snapshot -d 2      at most two levels deep
  snapshot --delta   only what changed since your last delta snapshot

Refs
  Write a ref as @e4; ref=e4 and e4 work too, and a number is never reused. A
  ref names one element while it stays on screen, or its twin the app rebuilt
  in its place, as (@e4 now @e9) says; else stale_ref: snapshot again.

Selectors
  Where a ref goes, a selector works too: #login-submit (test id),
  text=Sign in (name or value contains it), text==Row 1 (the whole name
  or value), role=switch, or bare text. A selector waits up to the timeout
  for a match, in view or not; a match that is covered or out of view is
  refused at once, as its ref would be. Refs never wait.

Acting
  click @e4 | tap 200 480     tap a ref, a selector, or a point in points
  click @e4 --hold 800        long press
  fill @e2 agent              replace a field's text, then read it back; keys
                              it took wrong go in again whole; a warning says so
  type @e2 ' smith'           add key events; without a ref, the focused field
  press Enter | Back | Home   keys; join modifiers with +, as Control+a
  scroll down 600             move the content; -s @e7 scrolls inside @e7;
                              strokes keep off the keyboard and system bars;
                              it answers once the content rests, or after 3 s
  scroll down --until '#row-150'   scroll until the element is mostly in view;
                              no stroke carries it past unseen
  swipe up | swipe X1 Y1 X2 Y2   move the finger
  open com.example.app        launch an app and leave it in front, else fail
                              not_in_front; close stops it and frees the target
  A tap goes to the part of an element you can see, and warns when that is not
  most of it. Its reply names what it hit, hit button "Sign in", hit text
  "Address" (no ref) without a ref, the labeled one under a view with no label,
  warning when that is not what you named. element_covered: less than 8 points
  show from under its cover, named as hints name it: a dialog, a button, also
  disabled, a bar or the keyboard; act on a dialog or scroll out from under.
  element_offscreen: none of it in view; close the keyboard or scroll.
  fill's and type's replies say what they read back: verified: text, or
  length for a masked field, from the field's value, a code field's value
  counting only when the text changed it; readFrom: descendants from its boxes,
  readFrom: name from the part of its name the text changed; verified: false
  and reason: no_value_to_read when the field shows nothing to read: look at
  a screenshot. An id on a field's wrapper fills the field inside it; on a
  view holding none, fill and type tap it and the keyboard it brings up takes
  the text. method: keys means typed, direct entered in one piece: set on
  Android and iPhones, pasted on a Simulator, which replaces the pasteboard and
  can show iOS's paste banner; fill closes the edit menu a lost paste leaves up.

Waiting
  wait --change               the delta once your last snapshot is out of date
  wait --change --frames      a frame change ends it too, for a canvas or map
  wait --settle               no tree or frame change for 200 ms, at most
                              3000 ms; settled: false names what kept changing
  wait --text Welcome         until an element shows it, naming it, alone first
  wait '#spinner' --state hidden   until it is off screen; also visible,
                              enabled, and attached or detached in the tree
  wait 500                    fixed milliseconds, a last resort
  click waits for its tap's first effect up to 1 s; wait --change gets one that
  may still be arriving or came already; wait --settle within 1 s of an action
  waits for its effect first, else says nothing changed within 1 s of it. Waits
  and open take --timeout MS: the session's (set timeout 5000), else 10000;
  open fails with deadline_exceeded when the launch outlasts it: give it more.

Reading
  get text @e5 | get value @e2 | get box @e4   in view or not, as inView says
  diff snapshot               what changed since your last snapshot
  screenshot shot.jpg         the latest frame as a JPEG, its pixels beside
                              the screen's points, which click takes

Output and errors
  --json prints one object: {"success", "data", "error"}, plus "code" and
  "warning" when set. A failure exits with status 1 and always has a snake_case
  code, such as stale_ref, unknown_ref, element_not_found, wait_timeout,
  target_claimed or ambiguous_target; text_mismatch means fill left other text
  in the field, which its message names, and covered_by_keyboard that the
  keyboard covers what you scroll: close it (press Back on Android), then scroll
  again. Without --json, errors go to stderr. A warning, such as a degraded
  snapshot, says to check the screen before you trust what is missing from it.

Targets, sessions and batch
  devices lists the targets. With several, pass --device ID or --platform.
  --session NAME keeps target claims and refs apart. A target that another
  session holds is refused with target_claimed, unless you pass --force.
  close --keep frees the target and leaves the app running for the next
  session. cancel ends your session's command in flight, such as a wait.
  batch --bail 'open com.example.app' 'snapshot -i' runs commands in order,
  stopping at the first failure; with --json, one array of results.

iPhones
  A phone on USB is a target when MOBILE_AGENT_DRIVERS names ios. A command on
  one that is not ready fails with its state as the code: device_locked (unlock
  it), developer_mode_off, not_trusted, disk_image_unmounted,
  no_signing_identity or automation_not_enabled (approve Enable UI Automation);
  devices and doctor name the step. It is slower: a tree read of a 500-cell
  screen takes about 0.5 s and frames come 19 a second, so settle and waits take
  longer. Text goes in through the runner, typed or set, not a paste.
